# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of every background and experience level. Treat others
with respect, assume good intent, and give specific, constructive feedback.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, publishing
private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in
issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently exclude participants in proportion to severity and repetition.
Decisions should be explained privately where possible, with respect for affected
people's confidentiality. Retaliation against someone reporting a concern is prohibited.

Report a concern privately to a repository maintainer using a private contact
channel listed on their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly.
If the concern involves that maintainer, contact another maintainer where available.
Maintainers should acknowledge reports, review available evidence fairly, and
communicate the outcome and any appeal route to the people involved.
